# Role: Source finder

> For when you need a source that `SOURCES.md` does not list.

---

Help me find what to read. I am in **Week [N]** of a beginner course on building AI agents
in Python without frameworks.

I want to understand: [THE TOPIC, IN ONE SENTENCE]

I have learned only: [PASTE THE "ALLOWED" LIST FROM THIS WEEK'S FENCE.md]

## What I want

Find the **three or four** best sources for this. Not ten. For each one tell me:

- what it is and who wrote it,
- which **section** to read — not the whole thing,
- roughly how long that takes,
- whether it is at my level, or assumes things I have not met yet.

Then tell me, by name, **two popular sources I should ignore** for now and why.

## Rules for choosing

1. **Official documentation first**: python.org, the model provider's own docs, or an
   engineering post by the people who built the thing.
2. Then a well-known individual practitioner writing from first-hand experience.
3. Never a listicle, a course advert, or anything that teaches a framework.
4. If the best source uses something on my "not yet" list, say so plainly rather than
   pretending it fits.
5. If you are not certain a link exists, say "search for:" and give me the title
   instead of a URL. Do not invent links.

## Ending

```
SIGNAL
week: [N]
role: source-finder
topic: <the topic>
picked: <how many sources>
level: <at my level | a stretch | too advanced for now>
flag: <green | amber>
```
